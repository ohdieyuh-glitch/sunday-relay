# TEA01 -- glossy porcelain for MI_V2_Ceramic: tried, REJECTED (2026-09-16)

Gap: the Tea Terraces landmark (LMTeaTerraces_Teapot, SM_teapot_pavilion, 13 x 17.6 x 10 m) and its cups read as
untextured white plaster at TeapotCam24; the Bible's teapots are porcelain and gold.

MI_V2_Ceramic is shared: 80 garden urns, 6 tea pots, 25 cups, 19 saucers, the teapot pavilion, the lantern swags,
two fountain/spire finials -- and the ENAMEL FACE of WL_Object_PocketClock (the hero clock at the arrival).

Variants (Roughness 0.22 -> 0.12, Specular 0.55 -> 0.7, TexScale 0.006 -> 0.0006), in memory, reverted from
record.json: c1 white (1.0, 0.97, 0.95), c2 blush (1.0, 0.84, 0.89), c3 pale blue (0.80, 0.84, 1.0).

Judgement: worse in all three. Gloss exposes the pavilion's low-poly facets, so it reads as crumpled foil mirroring
the sky, not porcelain; and the hero clock's face darkens or tints with it. The matte original hides the facets.
The teapot's problem is its geometry, not its colour: it needs a smooth, gold-banded porcelain form (forge objects
family), not a material tune.
