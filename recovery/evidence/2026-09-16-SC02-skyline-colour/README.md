# SC02 -- the skyline towers get their colour (2026-09-16)

## The gap

Re-surveying against BOARD_2 after SF01: the largest remaining difference is not at street level any more. The board's
city is castles -- tiered towers of pink stone, lavender stone and cream stucco under contrasting cones. From every raised
camera ours read as a field of hundreds of white pillars (`sv3_b.jpg`), which the board's own design rules reject
("no uniform repetition", "no cheap procedural look").

## What it is made of (`skyline_census_8m.json`, skyline_census.py new)

Above 8 m and excluding clouds, **MI_V2_IvoryStone paints ~47% of the skyline**, almost all of it on WL_Tower_* kit
modules from SKY02/SKY04/SKY05; pink and lavender roofs together are under 5%. IvoryStone is also the hero corridor's
stone, so recolouring the material world-wide was ruled out.

## The change

kitstack.Batches gains `tinted=True`: each stacked tower draws ONE tint for its whole height from a hash of its position
-- cream stucco 30%, soft pink 30% (lavender cones), lavender 25% (rose cones), ivory 15% as the accent. Three new
instances duplicated from IvoryStone (make_tower_materials.py). The three skyline scripts regenerated byte-identically
before the change, so recolouring moved no tower.

## Result (`sc02_ab.jpg`, `sc02_ab2.jpg`)

A real, modest gain. The distant metropolis now reads as cream, pink and lavender castles (SkylineRiseCam21, WorldCam11,
SW_Overview47's background); the hero camera is unchanged. It does NOT touch the near and mid-distance white towers in
the overview frames -- those are generator castle towers on MQ05's ashlar materials, the next target.

Map and the three tower materials saved.
